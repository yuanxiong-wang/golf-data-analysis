# GKZiD WIAA Social Sprint

- Created: 2026-06-21
- Wedge: WIAA / US high school golf
- Why this wedge: 325 player rows, 50 team rows, 5 events, 109 school/affiliation signals
- Goal: turn GKLive.TV moments into GKZiD card starts, completed cards, and shares

## Positioning

GKLive.TV captures the moment. GKZiD gives the athlete a home for that story.

Campaign line:

> You made the leaderboard. Now build the card that tells your story.

## Daily Caption Templates

### Clip

> Big moments deserve more than a box score. GKLive.TV captures the moment. GKZiD gives it a home. Build your athlete card.

### Card Reveal

> From leaderboard to player identity. Your score tells what happened. Your GKZiD card tells who you are.

### Community Prompt

> Tag an athlete, coach, or sports parent who should see this.

## Metrics

Track only these:

- Card starts
- Completed cards
- Shares/referrals

Decision rule after 14 days:

- If card starts rise: make more card examples.
- If completions lag: simplify the builder flow or create a done-for-you card offer.
- If shares lag: improve the end frame and add teammate challenge copy.
- If team prompts win: sell roster card drops first.

## Do Not Do Yet

- Do not expand to every sport at once.
- Do not build a complicated influencer program.
- Do not mass-message minors.
- Do not feature real athlete cards without permission.
